♚ Our Story

A Legacy of
Royal Cuisine

Nestled in the heart of Ore Chakand, Gaya, Bihar, Royal Restaurant has been a cherished culinary destination for over two decades. Founded with a vision to bring the grandeur of Maharaja dining to every guest, we blend royal traditions with the finest local flavours.

Our chefs, trained in the art of Bihari and Mughal cuisine, hand-select the freshest ingredients daily. From aromatic biryanis to melt-in-your-mouth kebabs, every dish is a tribute to Bihar's rich gastronomic heritage.

Step inside and be transported to an era of elegance — golden chandeliers shimmer, silk curtains sway, and every meal is a celebration.
